## Funding Strategy — How the Layers Stack

Three independent sources. MOE and NTU internal scholarship can't be combined (pick one), but RA funding from Chen is additional to either.

| Source | Monthly | Tuition/semester | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOE Taiwan Scholarship | NT$20,000 | NT$40,000 | Apply via TECRO HCMC, Feb–Mar 2027 |
| NTU Internal Scholarship (CSIE) | NT$15,000 | NT$65,000 | Auto-considered when you apply to NTU |
| RA stipend from Chen | ~NT$10,000–15,000 | — | He funds students he wants as RAs |

**Which to prioritize:**
- MOE gives higher monthly stipend (NT$20,000 vs NT$15,000)
- NTU internal gives better tuition coverage (NT$65,000 vs NT$40,000 — NTU CSIE tuition is ~NT$50,000–60,000/semester so NTU internal effectively covers all tuition)
- If Chen takes you as RA: MOE (NT$20,000) + RA (~NT$12,000) = **NT$32,000/month** (~USD $1,000). Livable in Taipei (rent NT$10,000–15,000/month for a single room).

**The condition:** RA funding requires Chen to want to work with you. That's what the portfolio project and cold email are for. MOE scholarship you can win on academic merit alone.

## MOE Taiwan Scholarship

**Website:** https://taiwanscholarship.moe.gov.tw
**Apply through:** TECRO Vietnam, Ho Chi Minh City
**TECRO address:** 336 Đường Nguyễn Trãi, Quận 1, TP.HCM

**Award:**
- NT$20,000/month stipend
- NT$40,000/semester tuition subsidy
- Duration: 2 years (Master's cap)
- Window: February–March each year

**Documents needed:**
- [ ] Application form (download from MOE website)
- [ ] Scholarship-specific SOP (different from NTU SOP)
- [ ] Academic transcripts (notarized + translated)
- [ ] Passport copy
- [ ] Language certificate (IELTS or equivalent)
- [ ] Recommendation letters (same as NTU application)
