## Mathematics main: sequential assurance for a finite access review with noisy reviewers

**Question and target.** Can a randomized review certify that fewer than τ of N fixed grants violate a prespecified authorization rubric, despite imperfect review? Freeze the grant population and latent binary labels Y_i before sampling; θ=N⁻¹ΣY_i. This target concerns rubric-defined legitimacy, not breach probability. A changing workforce or rubric requires a new campaign.

**Precise sampling and error contract.** Begin with replacement. At draw t, choose grant I_t with predictable probabilities q_t(i)≥ε/N, 0<ε≤1. Obtain a fresh review R_t∈{0,1}. Assume, for every grant and history, E[R_t|F_{t−1},I_t=i]≥sY_i, with externally justified s>0. Arbitrary fixed correlation among grants is allowed: randomization, not an iid corporate superpopulation, supports inference. Reviewer errors may depend on history only if this conditional sensitivity bound survives. Reusing a permanently mistaken recorded label after a repeated draw can violate it.

Set Z_t=R_t/[Nq_t(I_t)] and B=1/ε. Then 0≤Z_t≤B and E[Z_t|F_{t−1}]≥sθ. For testing H₀:θ≥τ, choose predictable 0≤λ_t≤1/(B−sτ), assuming 0<sτ<B, and define

W_t=∏_{j≤t}[1+λ_j(sτ−Z_j)].

Planned proof: nonnegativity and conditional supermartingale expectation imply **sup over admissible null laws P(ever certifying θ<τ)≤α** when certification occurs at W_t≥1/α. This is campaign-wide false-certification control, not posterior confidence, ARL, FDR or evidence about actual cyber harm. A budget cap can return inconclusive; non-crossing never certifies safety. Noisy without-replacement inference is not obtained by substituting changing q values into this formula: the remaining population differs from the original target.

**Proof scope beyond the product identity.** Reconstruct the relevant established [ALPHA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.02707) and [Shekhar et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.06884) arguments, then derive:

1. A reviewer-quality identifiability boundary: construct two latent populations with different θ and identical observable review distributions when no positive sensitivity lower bound is supplied.
2. A stopping-time result under the additional alternative E[Z_t|past]≤sτ−Δ. With fixed λ=Δ/(2B²), 0<Δ≤sτ, log(1+x)≥x−x² for |x|≤1/2 gives conditional log drift at least g=Δ²/(4B²). Bounded overshoot and stopping at T∧n yield E[T]≤[log(1/α)+log(1+λsτ)]/g, then monotone convergence. Derive a variance-sensitive refinement under an explicit conditional second-moment bound.
3. Counterexamples showing θ<τ alone does not imply that alternative: conservative s and false positives can keep the observed mean above sτ. False positives preserve the one-sided validity inequality but can eliminate useful power.

These are planned specializations of established sequential theory, not verified new methods. Large B or small Δ can make stopping bounds unusable. [2026 sequential-audit work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06116) remains a full-text comparison gate. If s comes from an external simultaneous lower confidence bound with failure probability δ, total error accounting is α+δ; average reviewer accuracy does not establish the required grant/history-specific condition.

**Application and pilot.** [Amazon Access Samples](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/216/amazon) supplies an anonymized entitlement structure and administrative history. Construct latent policy violations, risk scores and reviewer responses synthetically; never relabel removals as proven violations. Pilot four small scenarios, N≈5,000, 2,000 independent replications and a prespecified draw cap. At error probability .05, Monte Carlo SE≈.0049, approximately ±.0096 at 95%; larger final replication counts are conditional on timed feasibility. Measure false certification, stopping/censoring rates, repeat reviews and distinct grants reviewed; report capped review effort, not a selectively averaged stopping time. Runtime is unmeasured: time 100 replicates before projecting. Go if proofs are accepted and precision/runtime permit a useful boundary analysis, including unsuccessful certification. Switch if only the one-line martingale remains or conditional reviewer quality is inappropriate to the intended interpretation.

## Mathematics backup: exact-label, stratified finite-population IAM assurance

Remove noisy reviewers and use synthetic oracle-adjudicated labels under the same fixed rubric. Two prespecified role strata have known sizes N_h; sample fixed n_h uniformly without replacement, with independent sampling randomizations. Counts follow hypergeometric laws conditional on fixed violation totals V_h. Target remains (V₁+V₂)/N. Derive an exact composite-null test by maximizing the lower-tail probability over admissible integer totals; prove coordinatewise monotonicity by coupling and identify boundary allocations. Compare simultaneous stratum intervals with exact inversion and quantify budget allocation under rare-role concentration. This is a smaller proof/algorithm synthesis, close to [Waudby-Smith–Ramdas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04347), not a new audit principle. Pilot small populations to enumerate truth and verify rejection probabilities; scale only after profiling. It survives reviewer-noise difficulties, not rejection of simulation-based IAM applications or synthesis-level mathematics.

## FE main: how loading uncertainty and nonlinear valuation change a Treasury hedge

**Decision and observed target.** Choose duration, key-rate or three-factor hedge notionals for one fixed hypothetical coupon/amortizing cashflow book. Use [GSW fitted zero curves](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/nominal-yield-curve.htm) and model-marked monthly cashflow P&L, retaining data vintage. These curves are revised staff estimates; hedge instruments and costs are hypothetical. Separate cashflow ageing/carry from shock revaluation. The question is when estimated factor hedges leave materially more residual risk than simpler hedges, not whether a forecasting model wins RMSE.

**Analytic contribution ladder.** Reconstruct established discounting, duration/key-rate sensitivities and factor estimation, with [Diebold–Li](https://rodneywhitecenter.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/0404.pdf) as a nearest baseline. For fixed-dimensional factor exposures A and book exposure d, use h=(AᵀA+ηI)⁻¹Aᵀd, η>0. Planned theorem: perturbation of A,d implies an explicit loading-to-notional bound through the inverse norm ≤1/η; propagate this into residual linear hedge risk. Derive the uniform nonlinear remainder for P(y)=ΣC_jexp(−t_jy_j): under |u_j|≤r_j, bound it by ½Σ|C_j|t_j²exp(−t_jy_j+t_jr_j)r_j². Apply absolute net cashflows to the hedged book. Together these distinguish oracle residual, estimated-loading error and nonlinear valuation error; they do not claim statistical coverage without assumptions on estimated error bounds. The substantive output is a reproducible attribution analysis and hedge-choice sensitivity, not original pricing theory.

**Pilot and outcomes.** Use known Gaussian factor dynamics, then a declared regime change; compare exact discounted-cashflow repricing with linear/second-order predictions, oracle and estimated hedges. Prespecify four scenarios, initially 2,000 independent paths and common random numbers for paired comparisons. Report paired Monte Carlo SE, residual standard deviation, stress losses, turnover and assumed-cost break-even levels. Historical uncertainty needs dependence assumptions; synthetic Monte Carlo intervals do not cover history's unknown law. Avoid estimating 99% ES from a short monthly history. Runtime and download/schema feasibility remain untested. Go if estimation/nonlinearity attribution informs the decision and proofs remain interpretable; an unchanged best baseline is acceptable.
