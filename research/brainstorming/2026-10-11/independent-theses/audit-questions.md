## Mathematics

1. Are latent inappropriate-grant labels fixed for a finite population and rubric? Amazon grants/removals cannot supply those labels. Avoid computable policy mismatch being presented as costly legitimacy review.
2. For replacement sampling with predictable q_t(i)>=gamma/N and a fresh noisy review R_t, define Z_t=R_t/(N q_t(I_t)). Does EACH truly inappropriate grant satisfy P(R_t=1|past,I_t=i)>=s? Aggregate historical sensitivity or an AUC does not imply this. Reusing deterministic erroneous reviews violates the intended fresh-review condition. The idealization must be explicit; no internal bank data may be needed.
3. Under fixed H0:theta>=tau, E[Z_t|past]>=s theta. For M_t=product(1+lambda_t(s tau-Z_t)), can predictable lambda_t in [0,gamma] ensure nonnegative factors and supermartingale validity? Threshold 1/alpha controls erroneous certification EVER, not expected daily alarms or all thresholds simultaneously. A separate family-wide budget is needed for multiple certification targets. Is the no-crossing/budget-exhaustion outcome 'inconclusive'?
4. Distinguish clean-label weighted sampling WITHOUT replacement (Shekhar et al.) from the planned noisy-review WITH-replacement specialization. Do not silently transfer a WoR correction or claim reviews are unique. Report total reviews, distinct grants and repeats. Without a justified positive s, observationally indistinguishable populations invalidate latent assurance.
5. Is this enough mathematical substance? Require a thesis-level proof package (established foundations + noise assumptions/identification + a tightly bounded optional deeper result), not a new theorem claim for a one-line expectation. What exact adviser gate decides whether synthesis is enough? What nearest literature remains unchecked on noisy reviewers?
6. Simulation rejection fractions are diagnostics, not proof. For R=2000 and p=.05, MC SE is about .0049, 95% half-width about .0096. Use binomial uncertainty; do not call empirical 95% coverage evidence of exact validity. Score quality can change efficiency without changing fixed-population validity.

## Financial engineering

7. GSW is a fitted, potentially revised zero curve, not bond execution quotes or futures. Distinguish historical shock replay from a self-financing trading backtest. Fixed hypothetical cashflows are not a real bank book.
8. For curve-factor loading matrix A and ridge hedge h=(A' A+lambda I)^(-1) A' b, lambda>0, what deterministic perturbation assumptions yield a bound? Strong convexity, resolvent bounds and duration/convexity are established. Any estimated error radius needs its own assumptions; temporal dependence cannot be ignored.
9. Cashflow Taylor remainders require fixed maturities, bounded yield shocks and finite weighted cashflow sums. PCA/key-rate sensitivities must share units, conventions and hedge instrument definitions. Does the book/hedge gross leverage enter the remainder? Include it.
10. Monthly historical observations cannot precisely estimate 99% ES. Make variance/RMS/stress loss primary and ES95 secondary with sample counts. Compare models at matched hedge/leverage limits and explicitly assumed turnover costs. Define percent risk reduction relative to what baseline. Retain null/negative results.
11. If FE prefers discrete hedging instead, require full pricing/hedging/P&L conventions, expiry gamma boundary, assumed costs and simulated contracts. VIX is not an ATM put quote; Fukasawa already covers efficient discretization. What data/pilot gate makes that direction preferable to rates?
