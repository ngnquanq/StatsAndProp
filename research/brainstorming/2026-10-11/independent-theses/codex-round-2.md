# Codex Round 2 — cross-critique and repaired ranking

Read Claude Round 1 and evidence.md, then Claude Round 2 after saving this draft. The useful convergence is IAM assurance plus an independently financial risk decision. The weakest shared assumption is that public administrative records supply the latent outcome a security or financial model actually needs. A master's synthesis with substantial proofs and an honest application can be worthwhile; publication novelty remains unverified.

## Corrections that change the ranking

**Claude vulnerability M2 has a fatal label error in its present form.** The [official CISA schema](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cisagov/kev-data/develop/known_exploited_vulnerabilities_schema.json) defines dateAdded as catalog entry, not exploitation time. Counterexample: exploitation on day −90, catalog addition on day +10, no exploitation during days 1–30. Then Y*=1 while Y=0. Therefore Y*≤Y, the calibration lower bound, and the proposed one-sided underforecasting test are unjustified. [FIRST's FAQ](https://www.first.org/epss/faq) describes the operational probability through partner-detected/recorded exploitation activity in the next 30 days; [methodology](https://www.first.org/epss/how-it-works.html) already includes PU learning. These descriptions do not justify replacing its outcome with catalog additions. Repair to either future catalog-confirmation probability or cumulative ever-exploited prevalence by an endpoint. The former is observable but is not EPSS calibration; the latter needs explicit unidentifiable reporting bounds. Kernel smoothing and bootstrap cannot repair outcome misdefinition. My M1 survives only under that cumulative target, with broad-bound/power limitations stated.

**Claude IAM M1 has a better operational target than my policy-mismatch M2, but its proposed sampling theory already exists.** [Shekhar et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.06884), Sections 3–4 and Theorems 1–2, cover arbitrary randomized weighted sampling without replacement, weighted means, and prediction-assisted control variates. [ALPHA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.02707) covers weights and batches too; [Kato–Nakagawa 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06116) adds sequential finite-population auditing. Thus score-guided sampling and a probability floor are established ingredients. My reconstruction mismatch is fully computable once the policy and snapshot exist; sampling it does not substitute for costly expert legitimacy review. Public Amazon data supplies structure and administrative actions, not inappropriate-grant labels. Design-based randomization avoids iid-colleague assumptions, but only for fixed labels under a declared audit rubric.

**Both SOC proposals need a precise error contract.** Marginally valid conformal p-values are insufficient for arbitrary sequential betting: conditional validity or a justified conformal-martingale construction is required. Hour-of-week grouping does not establish that condition. A single e-process with threshold 1/α can control P(null alarm ever)≤α. [Shin–Ramdas–Rinaldo](https://nejsds.nestat.org/journal/NEJSDS/article/59/file/pdf), Theorem 2.4, instead establishes ARL≥1/α for e-detectors; its Section 2.3 explicitly distinguishes ever-alarm control. Neither is automatically a fleet daily expected alert count. A fixed daily fleet can use prespecified user/day budgets with valid conditional restart assumptions; perpetual monitoring needs summable budgets or an explicitly different ARL objective. ARL units must match hours/events. Keep SOC as reserve, not the strongest option.

**FE ranking also changes.** My generic shrinkage/turnover F1 is demoted: [1709.06296](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.06296), Section 2, already links transaction costs, covariance regularization and allocation stability; [1910.11840](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11840) is another close neighbor. A useful replication is possible, but adding turnover is no substantive delta. Claude cyber-loss F2 is rejected as the primary: records affected do not identify dollars of loss. Claude put-writing F3 is interesting, but data access remains a gate. The [Cboe methodology](https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/governance/Cboe_PutWrite_Indices_Methodology.pdf) specifies hypothetical monthly rolls, collateral and a VWAP convention; it does not promise investor execution at those prices. Dashboard access and the attempted historical CSV did not establish a usable full-history download. Index backfills, costs, collateral, total-return conventions and methodology changes need checking. Sparse crashes cannot identify jump parameters reliably; do not promise an optimal jump-diffusion allocation.

## Preferred repaired pair and contribution ladder

**Math main: finite-population IAM audit assurance under uncertain reviewer quality.** Fix N grants, a review rubric, an inappropriate-grant fraction θ, and a certification tolerance. Public Amazon structure plus completely synthetic reviewer labels provides an honest known-truth application. This is simulation-supported assurance methodology, not evidence of real organizational violations.

Minimum accepted thesis: derive and implement the established uniform/weighted audit procedures, prove their conditions carefully, compare budget and coverage under rare roles, score miscalibration and concentrated violations. The stronger narrow result is a reviewer-noise boundary, starting with replacement sampling rather than claiming immediate noisy-WoR validity. If reviewer sensitivity is conditionally at least s>0 for each inappropriate grant, I_t is drawn with predictable q_t(i)>0, and R_t is its review outcome, then Z_t=R_t/[Nq_t(I_t)] has conditional mean at least sθ. Under θ≥τ, nonnegative products of 1+λ_t(sτ−Z_t) are supermartingales for valid predictable λ_t≥0. Prove the certification bound, characterize its sensitivity to s and q's floor, and construct indistinguishable reviewer laws showing why no externally justified positive s means no latent certification. False positives can impair efficiency without invalidating this one-sided inequality. This is a planned specialization, not verified novel theory. Noise assumptions cannot be estimated from the same unadjudicated records. A WoR extension is optional work after nearest-paper comparison.

**FE main: loading uncertainty and nonlinear valuation error in Treasury-curve hedging.** Choose hedge notionals for a fixed hypothetical coupon/amortizing cashflow book, comparing duration, key-rate and three-factor hedges. Use [Fed GSW](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/nominal-yield-curve.htm) fitted zero curves and synthetic factor paths; calculate exact model-marked cashflow P&L. Those are genuine financial risk outcomes, with no claim of executable bond/futures trading.

Minimum accepted thesis: reproduce established factor hedges and [Diebold–Li](https://rodneywhitecenter.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/0404.pdf) forecasting, then explain residual versus estimation risk. Narrow deeper result: combine a spectral perturbation bound for ridge-regularized hedge loadings with a uniform cashflow Taylor remainder to bound hedging error under finite curve shocks. Assumptions: fixed maturities, bounded shocks/cashflows, controlled loading error and a positive regularization floor. Benchmark these bounds against oracle and estimated hedges in known-truth regimes. This is rigorous applied analysis with a consequential hedge-choice decision, not new pricing theory. Economic outputs: monthly residual-risk reduction, turnover, assumed-cost break-even levels and stress losses. Avoid emphasizing 99% ES from a short monthly sample.

**Backups:** Math: cumulative exploitation partial identification with sharp constructions, abandoning 30-day calibration claims. FE: covariance/turnover replication with explicit decision-risk attribution and a modest fixed-dimensional scope. Put-writing replaces the FE backup only after history/access validation.

Claude Round 2's added assumption A does not rescue a real-data EPSS claim: near-publication anchoring cannot establish exploitation activity in the forecast window. Its coupling theorem is valid only for the stipulated independent Bernoulli null, not for public KEV labels. Unequal CVE follow-up does not make endpoint prevalence undefined; it changes its population interpretation. Time-to-listing survival estimation additionally needs censoring/selection assumptions.

The main pair has separate labels, datasets, theorems and manuscripts: audit certification versus hedge-risk attribution. Transfer proof discipline, sensitivity analysis and known-truth simulation. Allow 6–12 months for math; FE proceeds later under conditional WQU 690/team approval, with a comparative-review adaptation for 692. Advisor acceptance of the synthesis-level contribution is the first gate, not an assumption. No experiment was run.
