## Final theme and two investigation tracks

Use the theme **when available data can support a financial risk decision**. It organizes the program without claiming a universal method.

**Track 1: minimum maturity for an interpretable cohort-risk trigger.** Ask when a specified 12-month serious-delinquency trigger can be resolved from partial follow-up under explicitly bounded transport assumptions. Characterize the required follow-up and sampling precision, rather than proposing another default predictor. Compare against established vintage/maturity adjustments and competing-risk approaches. The candidate delta is a decision-specific identification/uncertainty result; we must locate whether existing work already supplies it.

Keep the first pilot to artificial truncation of a matured cohort. Prepayment is part of the incidence definition, not an added regime-shift experiment. A transport specification must describe incidence or both competing hazards; past calibration cannot guarantee future transport. Choose sample size from observed/plausible event counts and declared precision, including any subgroup requirements. If counts are inadequate, expand permissible samples, simplify the question, or report the precision limitation. Do not promise adequacy from loan count alone.

**Track 2: ES replication and contribution audit.** Reproduce a tightly specified overlap/scaling comparison with known Gaussian truth. Check confidence uncertainty and the declared through-cycle exposure-budget loss. Build a paper-by-paper matrix: estimand, overlap treatment, sampling model, interval guarantee, decision loss, and finite-sample recommendation. Only a missing, consequential item supported by an argument and advisor approval can justify an extension. Adding GARCH, heavy tails, contamination, or drift is not a delta by itself.

## Success, failure, and stopping

A baseline's failure is not novelty if already predicted or documented. Its success is not disqualification: it may establish sufficient conditions or show that an additional method is unnecessary. Publish all prespecified comparisons and precision limits internally. Do not choose scenarios to create a favorable contrast.

Stop or pivot for duplicated contribution, unresolved identification/access, unusable precision, or unsupported decision relevance. Continue with null findings when they answer a worthwhile question and meet the program's contribution standard.

Fallback synthetic work needs a planted population financial target, original-sample and release uncertainty, and a delta beyond existing methods. Degree separation, shared-resource permissions, novelty, and assessment fit still require direct program/advisor resolution. No novel thesis commitment is justified yet.
